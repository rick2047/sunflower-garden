## License comparison

### Incompetech / Kevin MacLeod — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

- **No-cost and commercial use:** Incompetech's licenses page labels its Creative Commons option “No charge”; the FAQ says the music is free to use. The track pages offer downloads. ([licenses](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/), [FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html)) CC BY 4.0 expressly allows sharing and adapting in any medium for any purpose, including commercially. ([CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
- **Attribution:** Required. Incompetech's template is `"Title" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) / Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 / https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`. Its FAQ says the credit should be easy to find. A visible credits/about panel on the story page is a reasonable place. ([FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
- **Editing/remixes:** Allowed. Incompetech explicitly permits singing over, chopping, splicing, compressing, lengthening and adding instruments. CC BY requires indicating changes; Incompetech also asks that credits distinguish its contribution from new material. Credit edits plainly (for example, “trimmed and faded”) and identify any added music. ([FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
- **Website embedding / redistribution:** CC BY permits copying and redistribution in any medium. There is no Pixabay-style “standalone” restriction, so the track can be bundled with the story and served to site visitors, provided attribution and other license terms are met. Do not imply Kevin MacLeod endorses the story or add legal/technical restrictions that prevent others exercising CC BY rights in the music. ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
- **Content ID:** Incompetech says its music is being pre-registered with YouTube Content ID, so an automated claim may still occur. Its instructions are to add the credit to the video description **before** disputing and use the track's ISRC as provenance; it says claims should be released within 72 hours. This matters if the story or a trailer is uploaded to YouTube, not ordinarily for audio served only from the story website. Keep the track page, ISRC and attribution record. ([Content ID guidance](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/youtube-contentid.html), [YouTube FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/youtubeFAQ.html))
- **Caveat / not public domain:** Incompetech calls the catalog royalty-free, but its FAQ explicitly says its music is copyrighted and not public domain (including recordings of some public-domain compositions). The free permission is CC BY 4.0, not a public-domain dedication. If credit cannot be supplied, Incompetech offers a separate paid standard license. ([FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html), [licenses](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/))

### Pixabay — Pixabay Content License (not Creative Commons)

- **No-cost and commercial use:** The exact Morning Garden page says “Free download” and “Free for use under the Pixabay Content License.” Pixabay's current Terms grant a royalty-free right to download, use, copy, modify and adapt non-CC0 Content for commercial or non-commercial purposes, subject to prohibited uses. ([track](https://pixabay.com/music/acoustic-group-morning-garden-acoustic-chill-15013/), [Terms, §§4–5](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/))
- **Attribution:** Not required; Pixabay says credit is appreciated. ([FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/), [license summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/))
- **Editing/remixes:** Modification and adaptation are allowed, but the result must be a new creative work; minor changes alone do not make standalone redistribution acceptable. ([Terms, §5](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/), [FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/))
- **Website embedding / redistribution:** The Terms prohibit selling or distributing the track on a **standalone** basis. They distinguish a raw/substantially unchanged file from a new work combining media, text and editing. Pixabay's FAQ expressly allows music embedded in a creative project and discusses use in apps/games and video; a narrated/animated story is a better fit than offering a music-only player or direct MP3 download. Do not use the track as a separately downloadable asset or as a substitute music library. ([Terms, §5](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/), [FAQ — media in a greater work](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/), [FAQ — Pixabay music](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/))
- **Content ID:** Pixabay's current FAQ warns that some contributors/distributors register tracks with YouTube Content ID, which can cause automated claims despite a valid license. It says a shield icon marks registered tracks; registered tracks may have a downloadable certificate. Keep the track URL, filename and license evidence; if a claim occurs, follow Pixabay's dispute instructions. Morning Garden's page text reviewed here did not show a “Content ID Registered” label, but that is not proof of non-registration—check the live page's icon/status immediately before download. ([FAQ — Content ID](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/), [track](https://pixabay.com/music/acoustic-group-morning-garden-acoustic-chill-15013/))
- **Caveats / not public domain:** Pixabay describes its content as royalty-free, not automatically public domain or Creative Commons. Its current Terms say only items published before 2019-01-09 are legacy CC0; Morning Garden's page dates it 2022-01-19, so it is under the Content License, not that legacy CC0 provision. Pixabay disclaims warranties that third-party permissions have been obtained, and its Terms say users must stop using/remove content if a claim relating to it arises. ([Terms, §§4–5 and 11](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/), [track](https://pixabay.com/music/acoustic-group-morning-garden-acoustic-chill-15013/))

## Recommendation and pre-selection checks

Start by auditioning [**“Morning”**](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN2300003) and [**“Frozen Star”**](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100356). Their official descriptions fit the gentle/cinematic brief, and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) clearly allows commercial use, edits and redistribution with visible attribution—an easier fit for a public static website that necessarily serves audio files. If avoiding attribution is more important, audition [**“Morning Garden – Acoustic Chill”**](https://pixabay.com/music/acoustic-group-morning-garden-acoustic-chill-15013/) as a Pixabay alternative, but keep it integrated into the story and verify its current Content ID indicator and terms first. This is a rights/description shortlist, not a judgment about how the recordings sound; audition before selection.

Before shipping any track, the owner should:

1. Re-open the exact track page, confirm the free-download control and current license/status, and save the page/URL, download date, filename, license text, and any certificate. Pixabay says its Terms may change; its current Terms were updated 2026-10-20. ([Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/), [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/))
2. For CC BY, place a readable credit and license link in the story's credits, and note edits. For Pixabay, avoid offering the original MP3 independently and check for the Content ID shield/certificate. ([Incompetech FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/))
3. Check the actual file's duration, loop/fade behavior, loudness under dialogue, and technical size in the application before choosing. Confirm that the story's distribution/monetization plan and any promotional video fit the relevant license; if the project needs zero attribution, exclusive rights, or a guarantee against claims, obtain a direct written license instead of relying on “royalty-free” marketing language. ([Incompetech FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html), [Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/))
