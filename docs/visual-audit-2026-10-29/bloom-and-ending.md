# Visual review: bloom and summer ending

Scope: frames 094–116 only.

## Findings

- **Bloom hand/flower interaction — high confidence that it reads unclearly.** At t15.50 (`100_event_g281.42_11-bloom_t15.50.png`) and t15.60 (`101_event_g281.52_11-bloom_t15.60.png`), the characters reach toward the center, but their hands remain visibly apart, with a sunflower stalk between them; neither appears to hold a flower. At t16.30 (`102_event_g282.22_11-bloom_t16.30.png`) and t17.10 (`103_event_g283.02_11-bloom_t17.10.png`), the closer framing puts stems, leaves, and the large foreground bloom across the same central area, making any contact harder to read. This could be an intentional near-touch, and the foreground occlusion may be deliberate, but a handoff or shared flower is not visually clear in these frames.

- **Bee placement — visible, but plausibly intentional flight.** In frame 100 at t15.50 (`100_event_g281.42_11-bloom_t15.50.png`), a bee overlaps Anshita’s lower face/cheek; by frame 101 at t15.60 (`101_event_g281.52_11-bloom_t15.60.png`), a bee is near her reaching arm. Frames 102–103 at t16.30–17.10 (`102_event_g282.22_11-bloom_t16.30.png`, `103_event_g283.02_11-bloom_t17.10.png`) show bees near the characters and chair. The changing positions fit moving bees, but the brief face overlap can read as clipping or attachment. **Confidence:** high on the placements; medium that they are a visual issue.

- **Framing and overlaps — likely intentional camera push-in.** Between frame 101 at t15.60 (`101_event_g281.52_11-bloom_t15.60.png`) and frame 102 at t16.30 (`102_event_g282.22_11-bloom_t16.30.png`), the shot moves from a wider view to a close crop: sunflower heads and clouds are cut off at the top, and foreground flowers hide parts of the characters’ lower bodies and central gesture. Frame 103 at t17.10 (`103_event_g283.02_11-bloom_t17.10.png`) retains that crowded foreground. This may be an intentional emotional close-up, though it sacrifices visibility of the interaction.

- **Anchoring — no clear prop-float issue.** The potted flowers remain visibly planted in pots on the deck. The bees are the apparent floating objects, and their motion makes that likely intentional.

- **Summer ending and end-card continuity — stable hold after the cut.** At t28.00 (`114_event_g316.86_12-summer_t28.00.png`), the garden is still visible; the closer framing crops sunflower crowns and foreground plants cover the characters’ lower bodies, plausibly from a camera move. By t31.14 (`115_regular_g320.00_12-summer_t31.14.png`), the image is the black end card, which remains unchanged at t34.00 (`116_final_g322.86_12-summer_t34.00.png`): title and subtitle are centered and stable. The sampled endpoints show a garden-to-card switch; the transition itself is not captured here, and the cut may be intentional.
