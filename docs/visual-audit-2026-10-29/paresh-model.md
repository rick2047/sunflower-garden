# Paresh model audit

The model reads clearly as a deliberately blocky character. I don’t see a definite broken mesh or a clear severe deformation in these views. The candidates below are ranked by how much they seem worth checking—not by how certain they are defects.

| Rank | Candidate | Evidence and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Headband sits unusually high and forms a boxy arch.** The tall open rectangle above the crown reads a little like a handle or frame rather than a headband. It appears connected to the earcups, so this is a silhouette/readability concern, not an obvious detached accessory. | `paresh_standing_head-front.png`, `paresh_standing_side.png`; also visible in front and back. **Medium** confidence as an improvement candidate; the design may be intentional. |
| 2 | **One shoe has a conspicuous offset block.** The pale rectangular projection makes that foot look asymmetrical or split compared with its counterpart. It may be an intentional sole/heel detail, and the changing view makes its placement hard to judge. | `paresh_standing_front.png`, `paresh_standing_side.png`, `paresh_standing_back.png`. **Medium-low** confidence. |
| 3 | **Jaw-to-neck transition looks a little broken up at close range.** Brown rectangular tabs beneath the jaw and the small collar pieces create a fragmented outline. The close-up exaggerates this, and some pieces could be intentional neck or clothing geometry. | `paresh_standing_head-front.png`; compare `paresh_standing_side.png`. **Low-medium** confidence. |
| 4 | **Check the dark trouser lines if they aren’t intended trim.** Narrow, high-contrast lines run down the trouser fronts and reach the tops of the boots; they read as seams or straps, but are unusually straight. I can’t tell from these views whether they’re deliberate details. | `paresh_standing_front.png`, partly `paresh_standing_side.png`. **Low** confidence. |

The square eyes, angular face, mitten-like hands, broad shoulders, and chunky boots all read as consistent with the low-poly style rather than concrete defects. The chest straps/panel and rear pack are readable; the side view confirms the pack is a deep, boxy slab, but it doesn’t appear detached.

**Storm pose:** The raised arm and hand look connected in `paresh_kneel_front.png`, with angular joints that may simply be the intended pose. The lower body and opposite arm are largely occluded, so this capture doesn’t let me assess kneeling-leg deformation reliably. I’d treat that pose as **inconclusive**, not as a confirmed issue.
